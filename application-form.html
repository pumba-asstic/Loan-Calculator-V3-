Application Terms and Conditions

By applying for a loan you confirm that:

  • You are not currently over-indebted.
  • A court has not declared you mentally unfit.
  • You have not applied for, and are not currently under, administration, provisional or final sequestration, or debt review.
  • If you are married in community of property, or by customary or tribal law, your spouse has given you consent to enter into this agreement.
  • All information you give us is true and correct.
  • You will read and make sure you understand the pre-agreement statement that follows before accepting any loan amount you may be offered.
  • You will read and make sure you understand the Loan Terms and Conditions before you accept any specific terms in your quotation.
  • All applications are subject to Lift Off Lending's credit policy and an affordability assessment, as well as the submission of your SA ID and 3 most recent proofs of income.
  • We will email all your documentation and statements to you at the email address you provided in this application form.
  • You will read and make sure you understand our full Terms and Conditions before you transact online with us.

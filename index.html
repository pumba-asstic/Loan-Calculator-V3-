How it works

Four steps. No queues.

Apply from your phone, wherever you are. We keep you updated at every step.

1

Apply online

Fill in the short form, then submit your documents via email to admin@liftofflending.co.za:

  • Your SA ID
  • 3 months' bank statements
  • A recent payslip or proof of income
2

We check your documents

Our team makes sure everything is correct and complete. If something is missing, we call you.

3

Get your offer

We tell you clearly: the approved loan amount, your monthly repayment amount and the full cost with all fees upfront.

4

Money in your account

Once you’ve accepted your loan offer and completed your DebiCheck mandate, the money goes straight into your bank account.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who can apply?

You can apply if you are 18 or older, have a South African ID, a bank account and a regular income. You must not be under debt review, administration or sequestration.

How much can I borrow?

You can apply for R500 to R15,000, repaid over 1 to 6 months. The amount we offer depends on your affordability assessment. Use our repayment calculator to see what your monthly repayment could be.

What documents do I need?
  • A copy of your SA ID
  • Your 3 most recent bank statements
  • A recent payslip or other proof of income
How long does an application take?

The online form takes about 5 minutes. Our team will contact you within 24 hours to collect and check your documents. Once you’ve accepted the offer, you’ll need to complete the DebiCheck mandate, then the money is paid straight into your bank account.

What does an affordability assessment involve?

The National Credit Act requires us to check that you can afford the loan before we lend to you. We look at your income, your living expenses and your existing debt, using your bank statements and proof of income. We may also check your credit record. This protects you from borrowing more than you can repay.

What fees will I pay?

You pay interest, a once-off initiation fee and a monthly service fee. All of these are shown in your quotation before you sign, and they never exceed the maximums set by the National Credit Act. There are no hidden fees. See Credit Agreement Information for details.

How do repayments work?

Repayments are made strictly by debit order in equal monthly instalments on the dates set out in your repayment schedule. We’ll agree on suitable dates with you, usually in line with your payday, and confirm them in your credit agreement before you sign.

To help manage repayments, Lift Off Lending may use account-tracking services where applicable. Please make sure there are sufficient funds in your account on each debit order date. Your debit order mandate must be authorised before funds are paid to you.

What happens if my debit order fails?

Please make sure you have sufficient funds in your nominated bank account on your scheduled repayment date. If a debit order fails, we may notify you and, where permitted by your mandate and our collection arrangements, attempt to collect the outstanding instalment again. Applicable re-collection charges will apply as disclosed in your credit agreement.

What happens after three failed attempts?

If three collection attempts are unsuccessful, your account may be referred for arrears. We’ll contact you about the outstanding payment and explain the next steps in line with your credit agreement and applicable law. Further collection attempts will be handled in accordance with your authorised mandate and the applicable collection rules.

Can I settle my loan early?

Yes. You can settle your loan at any time without a penalty. You pay the balance owing plus interest and fees up to the settlement date. Contact us for a settlement amount.

What happens if I can't make my repayment?

Contact us as soon as you know, before the repayment date. We will talk through your options with you. Missed repayments can lead to extra default charges and may affect your credit record. If you are struggling with several debts, you can also speak to a registered debt counsellor.

How do I contact Lift Off Lending?

Call or WhatsApp us on 073 612 3221, or email admin@liftofflending.co.za. See Contact us.

How do I make a complaint?

Email admin@liftofflending.co.za with "Complaint" in the subject line. See Complaints for the full process and how to escalate a complaint.
